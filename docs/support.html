Common questions

Where is the app window?
Lumeth is an accessory app. It lives at the top of the screen around the notch and has no Dock icon.

How do I open the dashboard?
Click anywhere on the compact island. Use the chevron, click outside, or press Esc to collapse it.

Why is fan control missing?
Fan speed and control require a privileged SMC helper. Lumeth reads temperatures through the permission-free IOHID path and does not install a daemon.

Does Lumeth send data anywhere?
No. See the privacy policy.

Lumeth is an independent project. Not affiliated with Apple, iStat Menus or Pulse.
